About Me

I'm endlessly curious about what happens between the code I write and the machine that runs it.

Currently a first-year B.Tech CSE student at IIIT Hyderabad (CGPA 9.17, Dean's List), I spend my days building distributed systems, hacking on custom shells, and squeezing every last bit of performance out of multi-threaded code.

When I'm not debugging race conditions or breaking things in my shell, I'm exploring trustworthy automated programming, because if AI is going to write code, someone needs to make sure it actually works. I'm also building a multi-agent orchestrator system for AI-based analysis, which is teaching me more about system design than any textbook could.

I arrived here after scoring AIR 28 in JEE Mains (100 percentile among 1.4 million candidates) and AIR 1263 in JEE Advanced. But honestly, the most interesting journey started after I got here, figuring out how real systems work and how to build things that matter.

I believe the best way to learn is to build. Preferably something that breaks in interesting ways.
